# Day 23 - Embeddings Basics

## What are Embeddings?

Embeddings are numerical representations of text.

They convert text into vectors so that computers can compare the meaning or similarity between pieces of information.

## Example

Text:

"Python programming"

can be represented as a vector such as:

[0.21, -0.45, 0.73, 0.12, ...]

The actual values are generated by an embedding model.

## Why are Embeddings Used?

Embeddings are useful for:

- Semantic search
- Document retrieval
- Recommendation systems
- Question answering
- RAG applications

## Similarity

Two texts with similar meanings should have relatively similar embeddings.

A similarity measure can be used to compare their vectors.

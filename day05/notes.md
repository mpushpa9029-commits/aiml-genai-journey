# Day 5 - Role Prompting and Constraints

## Role Prompting
Role prompting means telling the AI what role it should act as.

Example:
"You are a Python Mentor."

## Task
Task tells the AI what it needs to do.

Example:
"Explain Python functions."

## Constraints
Constraints are rules that control how the AI should answer.

Examples:
- Use simple English.
- Give only 3 points.
- Give one example.
- Keep the answer short.

## Key Point
Role tells the AI **who to act as**.
Task tells the AI **what to do**.
Constraints tell the AI **how to do it**.
